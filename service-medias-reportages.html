Scope of work

A service structured around operational pillars.

01 —

Output formats

  • Photo and video reporting on energy sites, construction sites, yards, offshore structures and industrial infrastructure.
  • Models, visual materials, immersive content, operational sequences, interviews and institutional capsules.
  • Project communication materials, client presentations, onboarding, lessons learned, training or corporate documentation.
02 —

Energy & construction expertise

  • Understanding of field constraints: access, permits, SIMOPS, sensitive areas, confidentiality and HSE coordination.
  • Ability to translate technical disciplines into accessible content for clients, partners, investors, internal teams or institutions.
  • Sober, precise storytelling that respects complex operating environments.
03 —

International mobilisation

  • Teams prepared to operate on energy sites, offshore, logistics bases, construction sites and remote environments.
  • HSE qualifications, document preparation and coordination with local or client rules prior to mobilisation.
  • Production adapted to international contexts: Africa, Europe, APAC, Americas and the Middle East.
Objective: to produce professional media that highlights field operations while respecting the client’s HSE, technical and institutional standards.
